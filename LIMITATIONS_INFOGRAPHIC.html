Why Direct GB-Range Uploads Crash Web Pipelines
🛑
1. Browser Heap OOM
Converting a 1.2 GB file into Base64 creates a ~1.6 GB string in JavaScript memory, exceeding V8's string limit and crashing the browser tab.
⏳
2. Gateway Timeout
Uploading 1 GB over office Wi-Fi takes 8 - 15 minutes. Webhook gateways (Make.com/Vercel) automatically abort after 40 - 60 seconds.
💳
3. Make.com Free Quota
Make.com free tier permits 1,000 operations/month. When exceeded, the scenario is paused, returning 429 "Pipeline Error".

Executive Action Guidelines for Core Team
01
Audio-Only Extraction for Long Meetings
Export meetings as MP3/M4A instead of heavy MP4 video. A 2-hour meeting audio is only ~28MB, processing smoothly without memory strain.
02
Direct Google Drive Ingestion
For multi-gigabyte recordings, drop them directly into /meeting-audio on Google Drive to bypass browser upload limits entirely.
03
Make.com Quota Upgrade
Upgrade to Make.com Core ($9/mo for 10k ops) or configure a secondary webhook to ensure zero pipeline interruption during busy sprint weeks.
04
Smart Local Transcoding
Built-in FFmpeg auto-converts WhatsApp voice notes, AAC, and Opus into standardized 44.1kHz audio with zero user configuration.
